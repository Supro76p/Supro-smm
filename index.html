Questions and answers

How do I add funds?

Send money to the bKash or Nagad number shown on the Add Funds page, then submit the amount, your number and the TrxID.

Are deposits approved automatically?

No. Each deposit is reviewed by an admin, and your balance is updated after approval.

What is the minimum deposit?

The minimum deposit is ৳50.

Which platforms are available?

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Telegram.

How are prices shown?

Prices are listed per 1000 on each service, together with the minimum and maximum quantity you can order.

Can I use Supro SMM on my phone?

Yes. The site is designed to work on phone screens as well as desktop.
